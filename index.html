MyClock

A full-screen clock with local weather, alarms and a world clock — a display meant to be seen from across the room.

Getting Started

The clock fills the screen

On launch the clock owns the whole screen, with no buttons in the way. Tap anywhere to reveal the tab bar — Clock, Weather, Alarm, World and Settings — then tap a tab to open it. Tap the Clock tab to hide the bar again and give the screen back to the clock.

Choose a clock style

Go to Settings → Clock style and pick the one you like best: Digital, Analog, Flip, LCD, Dot matrix, Rings, Word clock or Minimal. All faces are original designs drawn by the app — none of them imitate another product.

See your local weather

The Weather tab shows the current conditions — temperature, what it feels like, humidity and wind — followed by today and the next ten days. Allow location access when asked and the forecast follows wherever you are; tap any day for hour-by-hour detail, including the chance of precipitation and the sunrise and sunset times. The temperature unit (Celsius or Fahrenheit) is in Settings → Temperature unit. Forecast data is provided by Open-Meteo.

Set an alarm

The Alarm tab shows a ring of all 24 hours: hours with an alarm are drawn thick, hours without stay thin. Tap an hour on the dial to create an alarm there, or tap Edit to manage the ones you have. Each alarm can be given:

  • repeat days — tap the circles to pick any combination of Monday to Sunday; leave them all off for a one-time alarm
  • a sound — ten tones to choose from, with a preview button to hear each one
  • snooze, with the length settable to 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes
  • a label, shown on the alarm screen and in the notification

Track other cities

The World tab is a world clock you page through: swipe left and right to move between cities, each with its own time and the day relative to yours (yesterday, today or tomorrow). New York, Beijing, Geneva and London are there to start with — tap + to add any city from the built-in catalog, and remove the ones you don't need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my alarm stop ringing after about 30 seconds?

iOS reserves indefinite ringing for the system Clock app. Third-party alarms are delivered as local notifications, and iOS caps their sound at 30 seconds — every alarm app on the App Store works within this limit. Snooze keeps working: tap Snooze on the notification and the alarm comes back after the interval you chose. If MyClock is open when the alarm fires, a full-screen ring takes over instead, with Snooze and Stop right there on screen.

My alarm didn't go off. What should I check?

Alarms are delivered as notifications, so MyClock needs notification permission — you were asked for it the first time you saved an alarm. If you declined, allow notifications for MyClock in iOS Settings → Notifications → MyClock (or from the app: Settings → System Settings). Also check that the alarm is still enabled on the dial — its hour is thick only when the alarm is on. And note that notifications fire on the device itself: an alarm set on your iPhone won't ring on your iPad.

The weather area says to enable location, or shows nothing.

The forecast follows your current location, which needs location permission. If you declined, you can grant it in iOS Settings → MyClock → Location (or from the app: Settings → System Settings). If permission is fine but the weather is unavailable, the forecast service may be unreachable — wait a moment and use Try again. The last successful reading stays visible offline until a new one arrives.

Is my location stored or shared?

No. Your location is used only to fetch the local forecast — it is never stored on a server of ours, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone other than the weather service the forecast itself comes from. The app works without location permission; you just won't see the weather. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why do I see ads, and can I turn off personalised ads?

MyClock is free and supported by banner ads, which appear on the Weather, Alarm and World tabs — never over the clock itself or in Settings. You can turn off personalised ads in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.

What happens if I delete the app?

Everything the app keeps — your alarms, your chosen cities, the clock style and other preferences, and the last weather reading — is stored on your device and removed with it, and any scheduled alarm notifications are cancelled. There is no account and no server copy: reinstalling starts from a clean slate.
